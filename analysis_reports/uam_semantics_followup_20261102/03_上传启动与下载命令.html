3．服务器完成后打包

脚本正常完成会自动生成下面两个包。若只需重新打包，确认没有同一矩阵父进程后执行：

cd /home/SY/uam/UAGMC-main
/home/SY/wyw/conda/envs/uam_server/bin/python -B -u /home/SY/uam/UAGMC-main/train_uam_stage_credit_18h_ONEFILE.py --resume --phase pack
ls -lh experiment_archives/uam_stage_stin_24run_60m_v1_DATAONLY.tar.gz experiment_archives/uam_stage_stin_24run_60m_v1_FINAL_REPLAY_MODELS.tar.gz
sha256sum experiment_archives/uam_stage_stin_24run_60m_v1_DATAONLY.tar.gz experiment_archives/uam_stage_stin_24run_60m_v1_FINAL_REPLAY_MODELS.tar.gz
